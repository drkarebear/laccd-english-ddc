Let us save one another from rebuilding the same worksheet nine times.

This directory links out. It does not rehost faculty files. That keeps ownership clearer, reduces privacy risk, and lets the person who shared a resource update or remove the original.

Nothing submitted publishes automatically. Resources appear only after review. Verification emails and moderator notes stay in the private Sheet.

Faculty are asked to share only materials they created, have permission to share, or can legally link to as already-public resources.
