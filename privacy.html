Privacy, plainly

Privacy and Data Practices

This independent faculty-created site is designed to collect as little information as practical. Browsing the site does not require an account, and the site itself does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or browser storage.

Important: this is not an official LACCD website. The site is maintained by Karen Crozer, Ph.D., on a volunteer basis. GitHub and Google provide some of the underlying hosting and form/feed services and have their own privacy practices.

When You Browse the Site

The public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not run its own analytics, advertising pixels, account system, cookies, localStorage, or sessionStorage. A page-wide no-referrer policy is used so external destinations do not receive the URL of the page you came from.

Events, Faculty Commons, and Teaching Commons load approved public information from read-only Google Apps Script feeds. That means viewing those dynamic sections makes a request to Google infrastructure. The feeds do not receive form submissions and cannot write to the private moderation spreadsheets.

When You Choose to Submit a Form

Submission is voluntary. The site links to Google Forms for Events, Faculty Commons, Faculty Commons update/removal requests, and Teaching Commons. Google processes those form interactions under its own terms and privacy practices.

  • Events: the private moderation record can include your name and verification email. The public event feed does not publish those private fields.
  • Faculty Commons: your verification email is private unless you explicitly choose to display it publicly. If you opt to display an email, remember that any public address can be copied, indexed, or scraped.
  • Teaching Commons: the private moderation record can include submitter name, verification email, permission/consent responses, and moderator notes. Those fields are not returned by the public feed.

Nothing submitted through these forms is published automatically. A human moderator must mark an item Approved = Yes before the public feed returns it.

What Can Become Public

Only fields intentionally selected for public display are returned by the read-only feeds. Event details, approved faculty profile fields, and approved teaching-resource information can appear publicly. Private verification information, consent records, timestamps, change requests, and moderator notes are excluded from public feeds.

Private-Data Retention

The moderation practice is to keep private information only as long as it remains useful for verification, moderation, or legitimate rights questions:

  • Events: private submitter name and verification email should be deleted or anonymized within 90 days after the event ends.
  • Faculty Commons: private verification information is retained while a profile is active and should be deleted or anonymized within 90 days after removal.
  • Teaching Commons: verification and permission information may be retained while a resource is publicly listed. After removal, private contact information should be deleted or anonymized within 90 days unless a legitimate rights or moderation need requires longer retention.

Corrections and Removal

Faculty Commons participants can use the update/removal form linked from the directory. Update and removal requests are checked against the original private verification record before a profile is changed. For other corrections or privacy questions, email Karen Crozer.

External Links

The site links to LACCD, individual colleges, Google Maps, Google Forms, and other public resources. Once you follow an external link, that destination's privacy and security practices apply.

Security Design

The public Apps Script endpoints are read-only and do not implement a public write endpoint. Approved feed fields are explicitly allowlisted, and submitted text is inserted into the site as text rather than executable HTML. Public resource/profile/event links are restricted to HTTPS before being returned for display.
